# Admin-Assisted Registration Guide

## Overview

Admin-assisted registration allows our team to create your account on your behalf. This service is ideal for clients who prefer personal assistance, apply over the phone, or need help navigating the registration process.

## When to Use Admin-Assisted Registration

This service is perfect for:

- 📞 **Phone Applications** - Applied over the phone with our team
- 🏢 **In-Person Applications** - Visited our office or met with a representative
- 🆘 **Need Assistance** - Uncomfortable with online forms or need technical help
- 👴 **Accessibility** - Prefer personal assistance due to age or disability
- 🤝 **Referrals** - Referred by a partner organization

## How It Works

### Step 1: Contact Our Team

Reach out to CEG United LLC through:

- **Phone**: Call our office to speak with a registration specialist
- **In-Person**: Visit our office during business hours
- **Referral**: Through a partner organization or financial advisor
- **Email**: Request assistance at contact@cegunited.com

### Step 2: Provide Your Information

Our administrator will collect:

**Personal Details:**

- Full name (First, Middle, Last)
- Email address
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Phone number
- Social Security Number

**Funding Needs:**

- How much funding you need
- Type of funding (Personal or Business)
- Business information (if applicable)
- Your current financial situation
- Any special requirements

💡 **Note**: You do NOT need to create a password. We'll generate a secure temporary password for you.

### Step 3: Account Creation

While you're on the call or in person:

1. Our administrator enters your information into our secure system
2. Your account is created immediately
3. Your funding application is submitted
4. You'll receive confirmation on the spot

### Step 4: Check Your Email

Within minutes, you'll receive:

- **Welcome Email** with your login credentials
- **Temporary Password** (secure and randomly generated)
- **Verification Link** to activate your account
- **Getting Started Guide** with next steps

### Step 5: First Login

1. Visit the CEG United LLC website
2. Click **"Sign In"**
3. Enter the email address you provided
4. Use the temporary password from your email
5. You'll be prompted to verify your email (click the link in your email)
6. Access your dashboard!

## Your Security & Privacy

### Password Protection

- We generate a strong, random temporary password for you
- **You should change this password** after your first login
- We never see or store your permanent password
- Passwords are never shared over phone or email (except the initial temporary one)

### Information Security

- All information is collected over secure channels
- Your SSN is encrypted immediately
- Only authorized administrators have access
- We comply with all financial data protection laws
- Your information is never shared without permission

### Verification Process

- You must verify your email address to activate your account
- This ensures you own the email address provided
- Verification links expire after 7 days for security

## Changing Your Password

After your first login:

1. Click on your profile or settings
2. Select **"Change Password"**
3. Enter your temporary password
4. Create a new, secure password
5. Save changes

**Password Requirements:**

- At least 8 characters
- Include uppercase letter (A-Z)
- Include lowercase letter (a-z)
- Include number (0-9)
- Include special character (!@#$%^&\*)

## Common Questions

### Do I need to be present during registration?

**Yes**, initially. Our administrator needs to verify your identity and collect your information. This can be done over the phone or in person.

### How long does it take?

**10-15 minutes** for the full registration process. Your account is active immediately after completion.

### What if I can't remember my temporary password?

1. Check the email we sent you
2. Look in your spam/junk folder
3. Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page
4. Or call us, and we can resend your credentials

### Can I register myself instead?

Yes! If you're comfortable with online forms, you can use our Self Registration process instead.

### Is there a cost for admin-assisted registration?

**No**, this service is completely free. We're here to help you access our platform.

### What if I provided wrong information?

Contact us immediately! We can update your information before finalizing your account.

## Troubleshooting

### Didn't receive welcome email?

1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait 10-15 minutes (emails can be delayed)
3. Verify email address with administrator
4. Contact support for email resend

### Can't log in with temporary password?

- Make sure you're typing it exactly as shown (case-sensitive)
- Copy and paste from the email to avoid typos
- Check for extra spaces before or after
- Use "Forgot Password" if still having issues

### Email verification link expired?

1. Log in to your account
2. System will offer to resend verification
3. Or contact support for a new link

### Need to update information?

- Log in to your account
- Navigate to your profile
- Click "Edit" to make changes
- Or contact your administrator for help
