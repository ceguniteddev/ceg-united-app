# My Accounts

**Route:** `/client/my-accounts`

## Overview

Connect and manage all your bank accounts in one secure location. View balances, track transactions, and verify your income for funding applications.

> **Note:** This feature requires bank integration service to be enabled.

## Features

### Bank Account Linking

Connect your financial accounts securely:

**Standard Bank Linking:**
- Link checking and savings accounts
- View account balances and transaction history
- Multiple bank support

**Income Verification - Bank:**
- Verify income through bank deposits
- Provides bank statement analysis
- Helpful for funding applications

**Income Verification - Payroll:**
- Connect payroll providers (ADP, Paychex, etc.)
- Direct access to pay stub information  
- Most accurate income verification

### Account Overview

View all your connected accounts:

**Account Information:**
- Bank/institution name and logo
- Account type (checking, savings, credit, loan)
- Account number (last 4 digits shown)
- Current balance
- Available balance
- Account status

**Grouped by Institution:**
- Accounts grouped by bank
- Total balances per institution
- Easy institution switching
- View individual account details

### Transaction History

Comprehensive transaction viewing:

**Transaction Details:**
- Transaction date
- Merchant/description
- Category (food, travel, shopping, etc.)
- Amount (color-coded: debits in red, credits in green)
- Account source
- Status (pending or posted)

**Transaction Tools:**
- Filter by date range
- Filter by account
- Filter by category
- Search transactions
- Sort by various criteria
- Export to CSV

### Account Management

Manage your connections:
- View all linked banks
- Select institution from dropdown
- Remove bank connections
- Refresh account data
- Re-authenticate expired connections

### Balance Summary

See your complete financial picture:
- Total current balance across all accounts
- Total available funds
- Balance trends
- Account health indicators
- Low balance warnings

## Security

Your data is protected:
- Bank-level encryption
- Never stores login credentials
- Secure authentication via trusted banking partner
- Session-based access control
- Regular security audits

## How to Link a Bank Account

1. Click "Link Bank Account" button
2. Search for your bank
3. Log in with your banking credentials (handled securely)
4. Select accounts to connect
5. Grant permission
6. View your accounts instantly

## Troubleshooting

Common issues:
- **Connection Failed** - Check bank credentials and try again
- **Account Not Showing** - Refresh the page or wait for sync
- **Balance Not Updated** - Use refresh button to sync latest data
- **Bank Not Listed** - Contact support if your bank isn't available

## Best Practices

- Link all relevant accounts for complete financial picture
- Use income verification for better funding rates
- Keep connections active and up-to-date
- Refresh stale connections promptly
- Review transactions regularly

## Navigation

- **Home** - Return to dashboard
- **My Funding** - Use verified income for applications
