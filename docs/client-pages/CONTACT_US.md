## How to Schedule an Appointment

1. Click "Schedule Consultation"
2. Select appointment type
3. Choose available date and time
4. Provide contact information
5. Add meeting notes (optional)
6. Confirm appointment
7. Receive confirmation email
8. Join meeting at scheduled time

## How to Request a Callback

1. Click "Request Callback"
2. Enter your phone number
3. Select preferred callback time
4. Describe your issue briefly
5. Choose urgency level
6. Submit request
7. Receive confirmation
8. Await call from support team

## How to Submit an Inquiry

1. Click "Submit Inquiry"
2. Select inquiry category
3. Write subject line
4. Provide detailed description
5. Attach relevant files (optional)
6. Set priority level
7. Submit inquiry
8. Track status in your tickets
