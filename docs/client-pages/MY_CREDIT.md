# My Credit

**Route:** `/client/my-credit`

## Overview

Access comprehensive credit reports from all three major credit bureaus. Monitor your credit scores, analyze credit utilization, and identify opportunities for improvement.

## Features

### Credit Score Overview

View scores from all three bureaus:
- **Equifax** - Current score and rating
- **Experian** - Current score and rating  
- **TransUnion** - Current score and rating

**Score Ranges:**
- 300-579: Poor (Red)
- 580-669: Fair (Yellow)
- 670-739: Good (Light Green)
- 740-799: Very Good (Blue)
- 800-850: Excellent (Dark Green)

**Score Changes:**
- See point changes from previous report
- Up/down indicators
- Last updated timestamp

### Credit Utilization

Monitor how much of your available credit you're using:

**Utilization Metrics:**
- Total credit limits
- Total current balances
- Overall utilization percentage
- Per-account utilization
- Visual progress indicators

**Color-Coded Levels:**
- Green (0-30%): Excellent
- Yellow (31-50%): Good
- Orange (51-70%): Fair
- Red (71-100%): Poor

### Tradeline Details

View all your credit accounts:

**Account Information:**
- Creditor name
- Account type (revolving, installment, mortgage)
- Account number (masked)
- Account status (open, closed, paid, etc.)

**Balances and Limits:**
- Current balance
- Credit limit (revolving accounts)
- Highest balance ever
- Monthly payment amount

**Payment History:**
- 30-day late payments count
- 60-day late payments count
- 90+ day late payments count
- Last payment date
- Payment status

**Account Timeline:**
- Date opened
- Account age
- Date closed (if applicable)
- Last reported date

### Negative Items

Identify issues affecting your credit:

**Types of Negative Marks:**
- Collections accounts
- Charge-offs
- Bankruptcies
- Foreclosures
- Repossessions
- Tax liens
- Civil judgments
- Late payment history

**For Each Issue:**
- Account name
- Type of negative mark
- Date reported
- Current status
- Impact level (high, medium, low)
- Age of issue
- Option to dispute

### Positive Factors

See what's helping your credit:

**Positive Elements:**
- On-time payment history
- Low credit utilization
- Long credit history
- Diverse credit mix
- Older accounts in good standing

### Recent Inquiries

Track who's checking your credit:

**Hard Inquiries (Impact Score):**
- Inquiry date
- Creditor name  
- Type of application
- When inquiry expires (2 years)

**Soft Inquiries (No Impact):**
- Self-checks
- Pre-approval offers
- Employment checks
- Account reviews

### Credit Report Summary

High-level overview:
- Total accounts
- Open accounts
- Closed accounts
- Total debt
- Total available credit
- Debt-to-credit ratio

## Credit Improvement

Get personalized recommendations:
- Pay down high-utilization accounts
- Address late payments
- Dispute inaccurate information
- Avoid new accounts temporarily
- Keep old accounts open
- Set up automatic payments

## Understanding Your Credit

**What Affects Your Score:**
- Payment history (35%)
- Credit utilization (30%)
- Credit history length (15%)
- Credit mix (10%)
- New credit inquiries (10%)

## Report Monitoring

- View current and historical reports
- Track score changes over time
- Select different report dates
- Monitor improvements

## Access Requirements

- Must be logged in
- Bank account connection recommended
- Credit report must be on file

## Best Practices

- Check your credit regularly
- Dispute any inaccurate information
- Keep utilization below 30%
- Pay all bills on time
- Maintain diverse credit mix
- Limit hard inquiries
- Keep old accounts open
- Review for identity theft

## Dispute Inaccuracies

If you find errors:
1. Document the inaccuracy
2. Gather supporting evidence
3. Navigate to Letter Stream
4. Create dispute letter
5. Send to appropriate bureau
6. Track dispute progress

## Navigation

- **Home** - Return to dashboard
- **Letter Stream** - Create dispute letters for errors
- **My Accounts** - Link bank for credit report access
