# My Business

**Route:** `/client/my-business`

## Overview

Register and manage all your business entities. Maintain accurate business information required for funding applications and link business bank accounts.

## Features

### Business Registration

Add your businesses with complete information:

**Basic Information:**
- Legal business name
- DBA (Doing Business As) / Trade name
- EIN (Employer Identification Number)
- Business phone number
- Business email address

**Business Structure:**
- Business type (LLC, Corporation, Sole Proprietorship, S-Corp, Partnership, Non-Profit)
- Formation date
- State of registration

**Location:**
- Street address
- Suite/Unit number
- City
- State
- ZIP code

**Financial Details:**
- Annual revenue estimate
- Number of employees
- Industry/sector
- Business description

**Banking Information:**
- Primary business bank
- Account type
- Account last 4 digits

### Manage Businesses

View and edit all registered businesses:

**Business Cards Show:**
- Business name
- Business type badge
- EIN (formatted)
- Status (Active, Inactive)
- Registration date
- Quick stats (revenue, employees)

**Actions Available:**
- Edit business information
- Delete business (with restrictions)
- Link business bank account
- View complete details

### Link Business Bank Accounts

Connect business banking:
- Secure bank linking
- Multiple banks supported
- Associate with specific business
- View business financial data

## Requirements for Funding

To apply for funding, ensure your business has:
- Complete business information
- Valid EIN  
- Business address
- Business type selected
- Business bank account (recommended)

## Business Management

### Adding a Business

1. Fill in business information form
2. Provide EIN and business structure
3. Enter complete address
4. Add financial details
5. Submit to register business
6. Optionally link business bank account

### Editing a Business

1. Click "Edit" on business card
2. Update necessary information
3. Save changes
4. Business list updates automatically

### Deleting a Business

1. Click "Delete" on business card
2. Confirm deletion (cannot be undone)
3. Business removed from your profile

> **Note:** Cannot delete businesses with active funding applications.

## Validation

Business information is validated for:
- EIN format (XX-XXXXXXX)
- Valid email addresses
- Valid phone numbers
- Complete address information
- Required fields completed

## Best Practices

- Keep business information accurate and current
- Use legal business name as registered
- Provide valid EIN from IRS
- Link business bank accounts for better funding rates
- Update information if business details change
- Register all businesses you operate

## Navigation

- **Home** - Return to dashboard
- **My Funding** - Apply for business funding
- **My Accounts** - Link business bank accounts
