# Broker-Assisted Registration Guide

## Overview

If you've been referred to CEG United LLC by a financial broker or partner, you'll be registered through our broker system. This creates your account and maintains your connection with your referring broker for ongoing support and service.

## What is Broker Registration?

When you work with a registered broker or financial partner:

- They help you apply for funding
- They register your account on your behalf
- You remain connected for ongoing support
- They can assist with future applications
- You get expert guidance throughout the process

## How It Works

### Step 1: Meet with Your Broker

Your broker will:

- Discuss your funding needs
- Explain the CEG United LLC platform
- Help you understand your options
- Collect necessary information
- Submit your registration

### Step 2: Provide Your Information

Your broker will collect:

**Personal Details:**

- Full name (First, Middle, Last)
- Email address
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Phone number
- Social Security Number

**Funding Needs:**

- Requested funding amount
- Funding type (Personal or Business)
- Business information (if applicable)
- Your financial situation
- Special requirements

💡 **Note**: You do NOT create a password. The system generates a secure temporary password for you.

### Step 3: Account Creation

Your broker:

1. Enters your information securely
2. Submits your funding application
3. Creates your account
4. Links you to their broker profile
5. Confirms your registration

### Step 4: Check Your Email

Within minutes after registration, you'll receive:

- **Broker Invitation Email** with personalized greeting
- **Temporary Password** to access your account
- **Verification Link** to activate your account
- **Login Link** direct to our platform
- **Your Broker's Contact Information**
- **Getting Started Guide**

### Step 5: First Login

1. Check your email for the invitation
2. Click the login link or visit our website
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. Enter your email address
5. Use the temporary password from your email
6. Verify your email (click link sent to inbox)
7. Access your dashboard

## Common Questions

### Who is my broker?

Your broker's name and company are shown in:

- The invitation email you received
- Your profile settings
- Dashboard header (if applicable)

### Can I change brokers?

Contact our support team to discuss broker changes. We'll work with you to ensure a smooth transition.

### What information can my broker see?

Your broker can view:

- Your funding applications and status
- Application documents
- Basic profile information
- Communication history

Your broker CANNOT:

- See your passwords
- Change your personal information without permission
- Access your bank account details
- View your full credit report

### Do I pay my broker?

No. Your broker may earn a commission from CEG United LLC on approved funding, but you do not pay them directly.

### Can I contact my broker through the platform?

Not yet, but this feature is coming soon. For now, use the contact information provided in your invitation email.

### What if I registered myself and now work with a broker?

Contact support, and we can link your existing account to a broker with your permission.

### Is my information shared with anyone else?

No. Your information is only shared with:

- Your assigned broker
- CEG United LLC administrators (for processing)
- No third parties without your consent

## Troubleshooting

### Didn't receive invitation email?

1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Wait 10-15 minutes for delivery
3. Contact your broker to resend
4. Verify email address with broker
5. Contact support if still missing

### Can't log in with temporary password?

- Type it exactly as shown (case-sensitive)
- Copy and paste from email
- Check for extra spaces
- Ensure caps lock is off
- Use "Forgot Password" if needed

### Don't know who my broker is?

- Check the invitation email
- Look in your profile settings
- Contact support with your email
- We can identify your broker

### Want to disconnect from broker?

Contact support to discuss options. We'll help you understand implications and make changes if appropriate.

### Broker not responding?

Contact CEG United LLC support:

- We can reach out to your broker
- Assign backup support if needed
- Ensure your needs are met
