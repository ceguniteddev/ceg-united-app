# Client Funding Products

**Route:** `/broker/client-funding`

## Overview

Your Client Funding Products page provides a comprehensive catalog of all available funding options you can recommend to your clients. Browse products, view detailed terms, compare offerings, and help clients find the best financing solutions for their needs.

## What You'll See

### Product Catalog

- Complete listing of all funding products
- Product categories and types
- Funding amount ranges
- Interest rates and terms
- Eligibility requirements
- Product availability status
- Featured or promoted products

### Product Cards

Each product displays:

- Product name and description
- Funding amount range (minimum to maximum)
- Interest rate or APR
- Repayment terms (length and frequency)
- Approval timeframe
- Key features and benefits
- Eligibility criteria summary
- Commission rate for brokers

### Product Categories

- **Business Loans** - Traditional business financing
- **Lines of Credit** - Revolving credit facilities
- **Equipment Financing** - Funding for equipment purchases
- **Invoice Factoring** - Cash advances on outstanding invoices
- **Merchant Cash Advances** - Revenue-based financing
- **SBA Loans** - Small Business Administration loans
- **Personal Loans** - Individual financing options
- **Specialty Products** - Niche financing solutions

## Visual Features

- **Product Cards:** Clean, organized product presentations
- **Comparison View:** Side-by-side product comparison
- **Filter Panel:** Intuitive filtering for product discovery
- **Product Badges:** Highlights for new, popular, or featured products
- **Rate Displays:** Clear presentation of rates and terms
- **Eligibility Icons:** Visual indicators for requirements
- **Commission Highlights:** Broker earning potential per product

## Search and Filter

### Search Functionality

- Search by product name
- Search by product type
- Search by keywords or features
- Quick search for specific criteria

### Filter Options

Filter products by:

- **Funding Amount:** Set minimum and maximum ranges
- **Product Type:** Select specific categories
- **Interest Rate:** Filter by rate ranges
- **Repayment Terms:** Choose term lengths (months/years)
- **Approval Time:** Filter by speed of approval
- **Credit Requirements:** Minimum credit score needed
- **Business Requirements:** Years in business, revenue minimums
- **Collateral:** Secured vs. unsecured products
- **Industry:** Industry-specific products
- **Commission Rate:** Sort by broker earning potential

### Sort Options

Organize products by:

- Funding amount (highest to lowest or vice versa)
- Interest rate (lowest to highest or vice versa)
- Repayment term (shortest to longest)
- Commission rate (highest earning first)
- Popularity (most recommended)
- Newest products first
- Alphabetical order
