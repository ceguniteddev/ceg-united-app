# Self Registration Guide

## Overview

Self registration allows you to create your own CEG United LLC account directly through our website. This is the standard way to apply for funding and access our financial services platform.

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Navigate to Registration Page

1. Visit the CEG United LLC website
2. Click **"Register here"** on the login page
3. You'll be taken to the registration form

### Step 2: Complete Your Information

#### Part 1: Personal Information

Please provide:

- **First Name** (required)
- **Middle Name** (optional)
- **Last Name** (required)
- **Email Address** (required) - This will be your username
- **Password** (required) - Must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character
- **Date of Birth** (required)
- **Gender** (required)
- **Phone Number** (required)
- **Social Security Number** (required) - Format: XXX-XX-XXXX

💡 **Tip**: Your password strength will be shown as you type. We recommend using a strong, unique password.

#### Part 2: Funding Application

Tell us about your funding needs:

- **Requested Funding Amount** - How much funding do you need?
- **Funding Type** - Personal or Business
- **Business Information** (if applicable)
- **Income Details** - Helps us understand your financial situation
- **Additional Requirements** - Any specific needs or preferences

💡 **Tip**: Be as detailed as possible to help us match you with the best funding options.

### Step 3: Security Verification

- Complete the reCAPTCHA challenge to verify you're human
- This helps protect your account from unauthorized access

### Step 4: Submit Your Registration

1. Review all information for accuracy
2. Click the **"Register"** button
3. Wait for confirmation message

### Step 5: Verify Your Email

After submitting:

1. Check your email inbox (and spam folder)
2. Look for an email from CEG United LLC
3. Click the **"Verify Email"** link in the email
4. Your account is now active!

## What Happens Next?

### Immediate Actions

- You'll receive a confirmation message on screen
- A verification email will be sent to your inbox
- Our team will be notified of your application

### Email Verification

- **Check your email** within 24 hours
- Click the verification link to activate your account
- Verification links expire after 7 days

### First Login

1. Return to the CEG United LLC website
2. Click **"Sign In"**
3. Enter your email and password
4. Access your dashboard

## Security & Privacy

### Your Password

- Must be at least 8 characters long
- Include uppercase letter (A-Z)
- Include lowercase letter (a-z)
- Include number (0-9)
- Include special character (!@#$%^&\*)
- A strength indicator helps you create a strong password

### Your Information

- All data is encrypted and stored securely
- Your SSN is protected with bank-level encryption
- We never share your information without permission
- Our platform uses industry-standard security measures

### Account Protection

- reCAPTCHA prevents automated registration attempts
- Email verification ensures you own the email address
- You can update your password anytime in settings

## Common Questions

### What if I already have an account?

- If you try to register with an existing email, you'll receive an error
- Simply click **"Sign In"** instead and use your existing credentials
- If you forgot your password, use the **"Forgot Password"** link

### What if I don't receive the verification email?

1. Check your spam/junk folder
2. Wait a few minutes - emails can take up to 10 minutes
3. Ensure you entered the correct email address
4. Contact support if you still don't receive it

### Can I save my progress and return later?

- Yes! Your information is automatically saved as you fill out the form
- You can close your browser and return later
- Click **"Register here"** again to continue where you left off

### What happens to my funding application?

- Your application is submitted immediately upon registration
- Our team reviews all applications within 1-2 business days
- You'll receive email updates about your application status
- Track your application in your dashboard after logging in

### Is my information secure?

- Yes, we use bank-level encryption for all sensitive data
- Your SSN is encrypted before storage
- We comply with all financial data protection regulations
- Your information is never sold or shared with third parties

## Troubleshooting

### "Email already exists" error

**Solution**: This email is already registered. Try logging in instead or use a different email address.

### "Invalid SSN format" error

**Solution**: Ensure your SSN is in XXX-XX-XXXX format with dashes.

### "Password too weak" error

**Solution**: Add more complexity to your password. Include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.

### Page not loading or slow

**Solution**:

- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear your browser cache
- Try a different browser

### Can't complete reCAPTCHA

**Solution**:

- Make sure you're not using a VPN that blocks verification
- Enable JavaScript in your browser
- Try using a different browser
- Contact support if issue persists
