# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals and small-business owners applying for funding or enrolling in financial education. They use the product to provide personal, business, and identity information accurately and securely, often while handling sensitive documents and unfamiliar financial requirements.

## Product Purpose

CEG United guides applicants through account creation, funding qualification, identity verification, and document submission. Success means applicants can complete the process confidently, understand what is required at every step, avoid preventable errors, and trust that their sensitive information is being handled responsibly.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, direct, and supportive. The experience should communicate calm competence, explain financial requirements in plain language, and make progress feel predictable without becoming cold or bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy or playful fintech styling, speculative-investment aesthetics, aggressive sales pressure, decorative complexity, and interfaces that obscure important requirements behind clever interactions. Do not make sensitive financial workflows feel casual, gamified, or promotional.

## Design Principles

- Make the next required action unmistakable.
- Explain sensitive requests before asking users to act.
- Keep system status visible during uploads, verification, and submission.
- Prefer clear, editable results over opaque automation.
- Build confidence through consistency, plain language, and reliable feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, meaningful loading and error status, reduced-motion support, and labels that remain understandable without relying on color or icons alone. Use concise language suitable for users with varying financial and technical literacy.
